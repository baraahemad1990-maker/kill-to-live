Kill to Live

The dead come over the fence in waves. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse and hold click to fire. Every wave you survive hands you a stronger gun.

WASD move · mouse aims, hold click to fire · R reload · P pause. On a touch screen, left thumb moves and right thumb aims and fires.
